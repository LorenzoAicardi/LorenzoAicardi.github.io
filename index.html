About

I'm a graphics and real-time systems engineer with a background spanning embedded rendering, VR simulation, and game development. I studied Computer Science and Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, where my thesis was an immersive VR training simulator built for medical education.

Day to day I work in C++ and OpenGL writing embedded graphics software, and outside of work I build renderers and real-time tools — most recently a physically based rendering pipeline in Vulkan, and browser-based demos in Three.js.

I'm drawn to the layer where geometry, light, and hardware constraints meet — whether that's a shader running on a headset, a rendering pipeline running on custom hardware, or a robot reading its environment through ROS.
